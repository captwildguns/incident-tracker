Incidents Launch Plan04
Background

Why we built this

Districts have run bus incidents on paper forms, phone calls, and email threads. Nothing in that chain records whether the right people were told, whether the district’s own policy was followed, or how the case was finally closed. Incidents is the module that closes that gap.
The design is settled. The prototype is complete and clickable, and development is building production from it on Tyler Forge for the November 25 release.

The problem we designed around

An incident gets written up after the route, not during it

01The driver’s job in the momentKeep 50 kids safe and keep the bus moving, not write a report
02Filed from a tablet, laterAt the school loop or back at the garage, while the details are fresh
03Every capture screen assumes thatThe system is built for an after-the-fact report, not a live event
04Not the tablet todayThe driver app is a separate Tyler Drive build running the same capture logic

Incidents Launch Plan08
The core idea

Every incident type carries a response path

01Response pathThe district’s policy for that incident type, written down as an ordered list of steps
02StepOne action, one owning role, a time expectation, and a notification when it becomes active
03ApprovalSome steps hold the whole path until someone signs off
04AssignmentType and severity choose the path automatically. Five types today, running 3 to 6 steps each
Incidents Launch Plan09
Worked example

Physical Altercation Response, 5 steps

01Immediate Driver ResponseDriver, within 15 minutes
02Submit Incident ReportDriver, within 15 minutes
03Parent NotificationSafety Coordinator, within 30 minutes
04Disciplinary Action ReviewAdministrator, within 1 hour. Approval required before the path continues
05Documentation and CloseSafety Coordinator, within 20 minutes
Every step names its owner, its time expectation, and who gets notified. Step 4 holds the path until an administrator signs off.
Incidents Launch Plan10
Per-student handling

One event, many students

01One event, many recordsAdd a second student and each child gets their own tracked record
02RolesInstigator, victim, witness, bystander, severity overridable per student
03Different pathsBecause the roles differ, the response path differs per child
04No busyworkA student who was only present gets no workflow at all
INC-2025-0059: one altercation, three students. Switching student changes the banner, the severity, and the assigned workflow.
Incidents Launch Plan11
Getting work done

The guided response

01One step at a timeA coordinator sees the step that is theirs, not a five-step form
02Plain instructionsEach step carries a what to do next checklist
03Sign-off stepsSteps needing approval ask for it instead of completion
04The baton passesComplete one and the next owner activates, with its own notification
Workflow tab. The assigned path, the current step, and who owns it.
Incidents Launch Plan12
Districts are not identical

Configurable, not hard-coded

01Workflow builderAdd, reorder, and retire steps without a code change
02Step templatesEach carries its notified group and email template
03Roles and permissionsManaged in-district, five groups out of the box
Steps, notified groups, and permissions are all configuration. None of it is hard-coded per district.
Workflow management. Six paths today, each editable step by step.
